Shrimply Study privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026.

Shrimply Study is a study app that runs entirely in your browser. It has no server, no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nobody, including the people who make it, receives your cards or anything about you.

Your library

Your courses, quizzes and cards stay in your browser (a backup in its storage), in the .shrimp files you download and in the .shrimp files you open from your computer, which Shrimply Study saves back into when you select Save, where your browser allows it (Chrome and Edge). They leave your device only when you put them in Google Drive, and then Shrimply Study saves and syncs them for you automatically as you work.

Google Drive

Using Google Drive is optional. When you sign in with Google, Shrimply Study asks only for this permission:

See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app (drive.file). It can not see or change any other file in your Drive.

It uses that permission only to:

  • save your library to your Drive as a .shrimp file, in a folder named Shrimply Study (made the first time), and save it again into the same file automatically, a moment after each change you make;
  • set that file to "Anyone with the link can view" when you ask for a share link, and back to private when you stop sharing;
  • list the libraries Shrimply Study saved to your Drive, so you can open one, or move one to the bin when you ask;
  • open Google's own file picker when you ask, to choose .shrimp files it did not save itself (uploaded, copied or sent to you); it can then see just the files you chose, and nothing else in your Drive;
  • check whether you can save to a file, and whether it changed since you last opened it; a library shared with you is checked about once a minute while the app is open, so it stays up to date (you can only view it);
  • show which Google account is signed in (your email address, read from Google Drive and shown only to you).

Your browser talks to Google directly. Each browser tab signs in with its own Google account. The sign-in token lasts about an hour and is kept in this browser's storage: in the tab's own session storage, so reloading the tab keeps you signed in, and the account chosen last also in local storage, so a new tab starts with it. Signing out in the sidebar forgets the account in that tab and closes its own Google Drive libraries there; it is never sent anywhere else. Shrimply Study does not keep, share or sell any data from your Google account, and does not use it for anything other than the features above.

Shrimply Study's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Share links

A share link holds the Google Drive ID of your file. Anyone with the link can open that library, without signing in. To stop sharing, open the file in Google Drive and set General access to Restricted, or delete the file. People who opened it keep the copy in their own browser.

To remove Shrimply Study's access to your Google account, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions.
